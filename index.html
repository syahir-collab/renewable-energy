Lesson 1

What is Renewable Energy?

Renewable energy comes from natural sources that are constantly replaced, such as sunlight, wind, and water. Unlike fossil fuels — coal, oil, and gas — these sources will never run out, and they release little or no pollution. This makes renewable energy a friendly choice for our planet's future.

🔑 Key Vocabulary

  • Renewable — able to be used again and again, naturally replaced
  • Fossil fuel — coal, oil or gas formed from ancient organisms
  • Sustainable — able to continue without harming the environment
  • Emission — a gas or substance released into the air
  • Resource — a supply of something useful, like energy or water

Lesson 3

Reading: Powering Tomorrow

Every year, more countries choose clean power instead of fossil fuels. Solar panels now cover rooftops in cities like Jakarta, while wind farms spin across open plains in Europe and the United States. This shift brings real benefits: cleaner air, fewer greenhouse gases, and new green jobs for engineers and technicians. Renewable energy also gives communities more independence, because sunlight and wind are free and available almost everywhere. Scientists agree that a future powered by clean energy will be healthier for people and safer for the planet.
